### USCG ACS Application and Auxiliary Member Status
- [ ] Completed ACS Application Form (ANSC-7069)
- [ ] Flotilla Commander Recommendation Letter, noting the following:
	- [ ] Basically Qualified (BQ) or Operational Auxiliarist (AX) membership status.
	- [ ] Good standing with the Auxiliary.
	- [ ] Current in all Auxiliary Core Training (AUXCT) requirements. 
	- [ ] Current with respect to payment of annual Auxiliary dues obligations.
	 - [ ] Not a subject of any investigation or disciplinary action.

### Ecclesiastical Endorsement 
- [ ] Statement of Ecclesiastical Endorsement, ANSC-7070 from an Ecclesiastical Endorsing Agent who is authorized by a religious organization (RO) recognized by the Armed Forces Chaplain Board (AFCB).

### Education or Waiver Request
- [ ] Education: Official transcripts or DD-214 if prior DoD Chaplain.
	- [ ] Bachelor's Degree.
	- [ ] Graduate Degree in the field of theological or related studies from a qualifying educational institution. A qualifying graduate degree program must require no fewer than 72 semester hours (108 quarter hours) of graduate-level work.

	-- **OR** --

- [ ] DD-214 and substantiation of Chaplain Commission.

	-- **OR** --

 - [ ] Waiver Request Letter with verification of the following:
	- [ ] At least one of the above required degrees.
	- [ ] Five (5) years full-time equivalent ministry experience.
	- [ ] Two (2) letters of recommendation from sources who have observed ministerial activities and capabilities of the candidate. Must be professional references. 

### USCG Recommendation Letters
- [ ] Sector, Air Station, or Base Commander recommendation letter. 
- [ ] Prospective Supervisory Chaplain recommendation letter.
